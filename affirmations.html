Foundation

Core Affirmations

These seven are the regular daily affirmations—the heart of the practice.

1

Divine Alignment

I am grateful to you for the divine grace and conscious awareness that align my daily choices, words, and actions with my highest spiritual purpose.

2

An Instrument of Love

I am grateful to you for making me an instrument of your love and grace to all the kind, caring, and loving souls you have surrounded me with.

3

A Channel of Grace

I am grateful to you for making me your channel to stream your love and blessings to everyone around me.

4

Healthy Body, Loving Mind

I am grateful to you for the gift of this healthy resilient body; a pure loving mind always aware that we are all connected; and an eye that sees the divinity and serenity in everyone around me.

5

Pure Thoughts, Sweet Words, Selfless Deeds

I am grateful to you for shaping me such that my thoughts are always pure and loving; my words are always sweet, polite and respectful; and my deeds are always selfless and noble.

6

Love, Dignity & Respect

I am grateful to you for shaping me such that I respond to everyone with love, dignity, and respect.

7

Radiating Love & Blessings

I am grateful to you for bestowing upon me the ability to radiate love and blessings to everyone I think about and/or interact with.
