Preamble

I am thinking of writing a preamble (to these affirmations ) that recognizes our encounter occurred during the week of Gurupurnima through the generosity of Dr. Jay and the follow-up by Shradha Jagasia Ji. We all know that Guru Purnima is a time to pause, reflect on our growth, and bow in gratitude to those who illuminate our path. In the spirit of Guru Purnima, I express my heartfelt gratitude to Dr. Jay for his warm invitation and visionary leadership at the Kerala Ayurveda Academy.

A true Guru or guide does not merely impart information; they create a sacred space where wisdom can be lived and shared. Dr. Jay has cultivated such a space here. In honoring him and this ancient lineage of Ayurveda, I offer these morning affirmations—not as a display of achievement, but as my humble contribution to our shared journey of living a purposeful, selfless, and dedicated life.

You may all recall that I also brought up ( and everyone in the classroom enthusiastically agreed) the warmth and positivity we all feel when learning in the classroom at Kerala Academy. I also want to express my deep gratitude to all the fellow students for the connectivity, engagement, and joy I experienced during our interactions.
